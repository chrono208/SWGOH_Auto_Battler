# SWGOH_Auto_Battler
Mouse mover and clicker.

<h1>Overview</h1>
This program will take control of the mouse and clicks in various places on the screen.

Explanation
Originally, this program was designed to automatically do the 500 battles required to complete the achievements in Star War Galaxy of Heroes.
Those battles were always extremely time consuming and I built this to solve that problem and fight against the constant grind that game developers made for no reason.

Specifications
This is made for the desktop version of the game.

How-to
1. Take your strongest team and time how long it would take for them to finish the battle and adjust the times between clicks to optimize it.
2. Adjust the pixels to make sure the mouse clicks where it needs to click.
3. Adjust the sleep timers to make sure there's enough time for your computer to move through the different screens.
